Privacy and personal data

Norte Retail stores customers' personal data in servers located in the United States and keeps it for as long as the account is active plus 5 years, as required by tax law.

Customers can ask to access, correct or delete their data by writing to privacidad@norteretail.example. Requests are answered within 10 business days. Deleting the account does not delete invoices, which must be kept for tax purposes.

Norte Retail does not sell personal data. It shares it only with the payment processor and the delivery company, and only the data each one needs to complete the order.
