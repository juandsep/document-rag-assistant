Payment methods

Norte Retail accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit cards, debit cards through PSE, and Nequi. Cash on delivery is not available.

Credit card purchases above 300,000 COP can be split into up to 12 monthly installments with no interest. Installments are only offered on credit cards, never on debit cards or Nequi.

Prices include VAT. An electronic invoice is sent to the buyer's email within 24 hours of the payment being approved; business customers must enter their tax ID (NIT) at checkout to receive an invoice in the company's name.
