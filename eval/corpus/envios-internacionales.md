# International shipping

Norte Retail ships abroad only to Ecuador, Peru, Chile and Mexico. Delivery takes 10 to 15 business days to all four countries.

## Costs and customs

International shipping costs a flat 95,000 COP per order, whatever its weight up to 20 kg. Orders above 20 kg are not shipped abroad. Import duties and taxes are not included: the customer pays them to the carrier on delivery. A package refused because the customer did not pay its duties is not refunded.

## Items that cannot be shipped abroad

Furniture, mattresses, lithium batteries sold separately and perfumes cannot be shipped internationally. The checkout removes the international option when the cart holds any of them.

## Tracking

The tracking number arrives by email when the package leaves the warehouse in Bogotá. After it reaches the destination country, tracking moves to the local postal service's website.
